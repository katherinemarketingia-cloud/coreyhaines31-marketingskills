# AÜRA Digital NJ Visual System Example

Use this reference with the video skill and the Magnific workflow when the active client is `aura-digital-nj`. It grounds the reusable visual framework in a specific direction for AÜRA Digital while keeping the workflow adaptable for future clients.

This is a creative direction reference, not a technical integration. Use ChatGPT for planning and prompts, Magnific manually for visual enhancement when useful, Canva for final editable composition, and a video editor for motion/caption/audio work.

## Client Context

AÜRA Digital needs a visual system that can support content and campaign assets already planned for the client, including:

- `content-calendar-30d.md`
- `campaign-architecture.md`
- `initial-ads.md`

Do not rewrite those documents from this skill. Use this visual system to turn their hooks, offers, posts, and ad angles into consistent static assets, carousel slides, ad images, reel covers, and visual support frames.

## 1. Visual Direction for AÜRA

The AÜRA Digital look should feel **editorial, modern, premium, close, and current**. It should not feel like a cold corporate template or a generic AI-generated design.

Core direction:

- Use a **real photographic background** whenever possible.
- Add a dark or translucent overlay/block to hold text clearly.
- Let the text be the hero, supported by the image rather than buried inside it.
- Use a refined blue/cyan system derived from the AÜRA logo.
- Keep the composition clean, with negative space and strong contrast.
- Make the aesthetic usable for both organic content and paid ads.

## 2. Visual Principles

- **Real photo background:** Prefer real business, team, laptop, website, local, or client-relevant imagery over generic illustrations.
- **High contrast:** Text must remain readable on mobile, especially in feed and story placements.
- **Blue/cyan brand world:** Pull from the logo direction: deep blue, mid blue, electric blue, cyan/turquoise, white, and dark overlays.
- **Text as protagonist:** Headlines and core messages should drive the layout.
- **Clean composition:** Use fewer elements, stronger spacing, and one main focal point.
- **Premium air:** Use editorial cropping, refined contrast, subtle gradients, and restrained effects.
- **Avoid generic illustrations:** Do not default to SaaS blobs, abstract people, generic 3D shapes, or unrelated AI icons.
- **Avoid decorative excess:** No unnecessary arrows, sparkles, gradients, shapes, or stickers unless they clarify the message.
- **Avoid obvious stock:** Choose imagery that feels lived-in, local, credible, or brand-specific.

## 3. AÜRA Typography

Use these as the visual reference system:

| Role | Typeface |
|------|----------|
| **Headlines** | `TAN Galand` |
| **Body / support text** | `CS Gordon Serif` |

Guidance:

- Use `TAN Galand` for short, high-impact headlines and covers.
- Use `CS Gordon Serif` for secondary lines, context, captions inside designed assets, and refined supporting copy.
- Keep typography editable in Canva. Do not ask AI image tools to render final text.
- If a font is unavailable in a tool, choose the closest visual substitute and document the substitution.

## 4. Color Guidelines

Build from the AÜRA logo rather than inventing a distant palette. Use an operational palette like this, adjusted after sampling the actual logo files:

| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| **Deep blue / navy** | Main background, overlays, premium contrast |
| **Mid blue** | Supporting blocks, secondary accents, gradients |
| **Electric blue** | CTA accents, highlights, active lines |
| **Cyan / turquoise** | Freshness, digital energy, small emphasis moments |
| **White** | Primary text on dark overlays, breathing room |
| **Black or very dark blue** | Transparent overlays, text containers, image control |

Usage rules:

- Use dark overlays to make text readable over photos.
- Keep cyan/electric blue as accents, not full-background defaults.
- Preserve enough white space so the system feels premium rather than noisy.
- When creating variants, shift emphasis between navy, electric blue, and cyan without leaving the logo’s visual world.

## 5. Application to Real Pieces

### Carousel

- Slide 1: real photo or enhanced screenshot background, dark translucent overlay, `TAN Galand` headline.
- Supporting slides: repeat the overlay system, use `CS Gordon Serif` for context, and include one clear idea per slide.
- Visual rhythm: alternate text-led slides with screenshot/mockup slides.
- Magnific use: improve a web screenshot, mockup, or background before importing to Canva.
- Final review: check mobile readability and whether the first slide feels distinctive enough to stop the scroll.

### Static Piece

- Use one strong headline, one supporting line, and one visual focal point.
- Choose a realistic background connected to the offer or audience.
- Add a navy or black-blue transparent block behind text.
- Use cyan/electric blue for one small emphasis element such as a line, button-like CTA, or highlight.
- Export feed variants such as 1080x1350 and 1080x1080 when relevant.

### Image Ad

- Prioritize the offer, outcome, or pain point over visual complexity.
- Use a high-contrast dark overlay to improve comprehension in the feed.
- Keep final copy editable for fast hook testing.
- Create variants by changing headline angle, CTA emphasis, screenshot choice, and background intensity.
- Avoid relying on subtle brand details that disappear in small placements.

### Visual Support for Reels

- Use the same visual system for reel covers, intro cards, stat cards, and end cards.
- Keep the background photographic or screenshot-based, with strong contrast and minimal decoration.
- Use the video editor for timing, captions, audio, and cuts.
- Use Canva for static frames and covers before importing into CapCut, Descript, or another editor.

## Magnific Workflow for AÜRA

Use Magnific manually when a base asset is conceptually right but needs polish:

1. Start with a real AÜRA-related screenshot, website capture, brand-relevant photo, or generated background.
2. Decide what should improve: depth, lighting, realism, texture, or premium editorial feel.
3. Keep the prompt constrained to the AÜRA direction: real photo base, dark overlay later, blue/cyan accents, modern editorial feel.
4. Export the enhanced asset.
5. Place the enhanced asset into Canva.
6. Add final text, typography, logo, overlays, CTA, and format-specific spacing manually.
7. Check that any screenshot, UI detail, logo, or visible claim remained accurate.

Do not use Magnific to invent AÜRA’s offer, claims, testimonials, WhatsApp flow, or campaign strategy. It only supports the visual layer.

## Quality Checklist for AÜRA

- Does the asset feel editorial and modern rather than corporate-generic?
- Is the background photographic, realistic, and relevant?
- Is the headline dominant and readable on mobile?
- Are the blues/cyans connected to the logo direction?
- Are `TAN Galand` and `CS Gordon Serif` used or approximated intentionally?
- Is the composition clean with enough negative space?
- Does the overlay make text clearer without hiding the image completely?
- Would the asset work for both an organic post and a campaign adaptation?
- Does it avoid obvious stock-photo and generic AI-template signals?
- Are campaign claims, screenshots, logos, and visible details still accurate?
